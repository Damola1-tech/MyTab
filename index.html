Questions.

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. They get a normal WhatsApp message with a link that opens a simple page showing their balance. Nothing to download.

What if there is no network?

MyTab keeps working. Sales, payments, spending and your nightly close are saved on your phone and sent when you are back online.

Can my attendant see my money?

No. An attendant signs in with a short code and can only record sales and see their own. They cannot see your totals, who owes you, customer phone numbers, or anything you owe.

What does "Short" mean at the end of the night?

It means the drawer has less cash than your records say it should. It is not an accusation. Wrong change, a sale not written down, or a supplier paid in cash can all cause it. MyTab shows the gap and helps you look.

Is the Shop Record a bank statement?

No. It is your own record of sales, spending and closed nights, kept night by night. It can help when you talk to a supplier or a lender, but they decide what they accept.

What happens to my data if I stop?

You can delete your account yourself, inside the app, at any time. Everything is removed.
