Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

MyTab is a record-keeping app for shops that sell on credit. This Policy explains what information MyTab collects, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. In this Policy, “we” means the team that runs MyTab, “you” means the shop owner, and “your customers” means the people you record in the app.

1. Whose data is whose

You decide what you record about your own customers, so you are responsible for that information and for having a good reason to keep it. We store it for you and only to run MyTab. We do not use your customers’ details for our own purposes, and we never contact them.

2. Information we collect

About you (the shop owner): business name, phone number or email, type of business, the 4-digit code that protects your account (stored only as a one-way hash, never as the code itself), the email you give for payment receipts, and your payment details for customers if you choose to add them (for example a bank account name and number).

About your attendants: if you add attendants, we store their name, a hashed sign-in code, and the sales and customer payments they record under their name.

About your customers: what you choose to enter, usually a name, phone number, birthday, what they bought on credit, amounts owed and payments made.

Your shop records: your products and prices, your sales, and what your shop owes to other people (names, phone numbers, amounts).

Your shop's money and nightly close: how each sale was paid (cash, transfer or credit), money you record as spent, customer payments you record, the cash you count when you close the shop, and a change log. The change log keeps the original and the new value, and who changed it and when, for anything added, changed or deleted after a night was closed. It is kept until you delete your account.

Joining our early list: if you use the form on our website, your name, WhatsApp number and type of shop, so we can message you about MyTab.

Payments: Pro payments are handled by Paystack. We never see or store your card number. We keep only a reference that tells us your plan is paid and until when.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell data to advertisers.

3. The balance link you can send a customer

When you tap “Send reminder”, MyTab creates a private web link for that customer. Anyone who has the link can see that customer’s name, your shop name, the items and balance they owe, and the payment details you added. The link cannot be guessed, but anyone you forward it to can open it, so send it only to the customer. Messages are sent from your own WhatsApp on your own phone. MyTab does not send messages for you.

4. How we use information

  • To run the app: keep your records, balances, sales and reminders, and keep them in sync across your phones.
  • To let attendants sign in and record sales without seeing your totals.
  • To process Pro payments and to turn Pro on and off correctly.
  • To message you on WhatsApp if you joined the early list.
  • To keep MyTab secure and to fix problems.

We use your information because you asked us to run MyTab for you (contract), because you agreed to it (consent, for example the early list), and to protect the service from misuse.

5. Where your data is stored

Your records are stored in a hosted PostgreSQL database (Neon) and served through our backend (Render). These providers run servers outside Nigeria, so your data may be processed in other countries. They are bound to protect it, and data is encrypted while it travels. A copy is also kept on your own phone so MyTab works offline, and it syncs when you are back online.

6. Who we share information with

  • Paystack, to take Pro payments. It receives your payment email and payment details, not your customers’ information.
  • Our hosting providers (Render, Neon), to run the app.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We do not sell your data or your customers’ data, and we do not share your customers’ names, phone numbers or debts with WhatsApp, Paystack or advertisers.

7. How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your records while your account is open. You can delete your account yourself at any time in the app: Settings, then “Delete my account”. This permanently removes your shop, customers, debts, sales, attendants and balance links. If you have a card subscription, cancel it first so it stops charging. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for a short time until they are overwritten. You can also delete a single customer or entry at any time. You can sign out of a phone at any time, which clears that phone’s local copy.

If you joined the early list and want to be removed, ask us and we will delete your entry.

8. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act you can ask to see your data, correct it, delete it, limit or object to how we use it, take a copy of it, and withdraw consent you gave. Most of this you can do yourself in the app. For anything else, contact us (see section 11). You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

If you are a customer of a shop that uses MyTab and want your details corrected or removed, please ask the shop first, because the shop controls its records. You can also contact us and we will pass the request on.

9. Security

Account and attendant codes are stored as hashes. Connections use HTTPS. Repeated wrong sign-in attempts are slowed down. Attendants cannot delete records or see your totals, and removing an attendant signs them out. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your phone and your code private. If we learn of a breach that puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Children

MyTab is for business owners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Contact

You can reach us through the form in the “Join the early list” section at mytab.com.ng. Say what you need and we will reply on WhatsApp. For privacy requests, write “Privacy request” at the start of your message.

12. Changes to this Policy

If we make important changes, we will update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app.
