Delete your MyTab account

You can do it yourself, any time, inside the app.

How to delete it

  1. Open MyTab and sign in.
  2. Tap the gear (Settings).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and enter your secret code to confirm.

Stuck, or cannot open the app? Message us on WhatsApp on 0706 865 5350 or email lawaloyindamolashinaayomi@gmail.com from the number you signed up with, and we will help.

What is deleted

Your shop profile, customers, debts and payments, sales, products, what you owe, your closing records and the change log, and your attendants. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you pay for Pro by card

Cancel it first (Settings, then Manage subscription). We cannot delete an account that is still being charged.
