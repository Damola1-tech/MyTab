Delete your MyTab account

You can delete your account yourself, any time, inside the app:

  1. Open MyTab and log in.
  2. Tap the gear (Settings).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and enter your secret code to confirm.

What is deleted: your shop profile, customers, debts and payments, sales, products, and what you owe. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you have a Pro card subscription, cancel it first (Settings, then Manage subscription). We cannot delete an account that is still being charged.
